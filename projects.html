LandingCA - Group Startup Project

A group project where we had to come up with a startup idea and build it out from scratch. We created LandingCA, a platform aimed at international students moving to Canada. The idea is to make the transition easier by walking students through things like getting a SIN, opening a bank account, filing taxes, and understanding health insurance.

I worked as the Founder and Product Manager, leading the concept and research. Nicolas Steinhauser handled development and Andrew Ferasol took care of the design.
